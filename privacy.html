Privacy Policy

The CoolBears website does not require a traditional account to browse. When you connect a TON wallet, the public wallet address and blockchain activity may be processed as necessary to provide mint functionality.

Blockchain data is public by design. Never enter or share a wallet seed phrase or private key on this website.

Hosting, wallet-connect, analytics or infrastructure providers may process technical data such as IP address and browser information according to their own policies. The final production policy will be completed before public mint with the final service-provider and controller details.
